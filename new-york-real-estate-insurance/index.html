Who is New York real estate insurance for?

Building owners, landlords, mixed-use owners, co-op and condo interests, and real estate investors in New York.

What can a New York building policy include?

Property, liability, loss of rents, and umbrella, when the policy includes them. What is covered depends on the policy.

Is a homeowners policy enough for a New York rental?

A home you live in and a property you rent are different risks. A rental usually needs a landlord or building policy. The policy that is issued governs.
