What is the Real Estate Program?

It is the specialty for habitational accounts: landlords, mixed-use buildings, apartment buildings, co-ops, and condos. It is our specialty practice, not a program from a single insurer. SimpleINSURE also places personal and commercial lines for other industries. What a policy covers depends on the form.

Do you place building insurance in Queens?

Yes. Queens is part of the New York work, with the other boroughs. Lender and lease requirements follow the loan, the lease, and New York City and state rules. The policy controls what is covered.

Is Long Island part of the program?

Yes. Nassau and Suffolk landlords and small buildings are part of it. A rental needs a landlord or building policy, not a homeowners policy. What is paid depends on the policy.

Does the program replace the other lines?

No. Personal and commercial lines are still placed, for many industries. Real estate is the specialty, not the only account.
