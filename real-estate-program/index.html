Who it is for

Landlords

Rental houses, two-family homes, and small buildings. A rental needs a landlord or building policy, not a homeowners policy.

Mixed-use

A store or restaurant below, apartments above. The building is usually a commercial package: property for the structure and general liability for the owner, subject to the policy. The business in the store is a separate policy. Mixed-use buildings.

Apartment buildings

Habitational property, loss of rents when the form includes it, and premises liability.

Co-ops and condos

The building policy and a unit owner policy are not the same thing. Bylaws and the loan can ask for both. What each pays depends on that policy.

What we place

  • Building property
  • General liability for the premises
  • Loss of rents, when the form includes it
  • Umbrella, over the underlying policies

New York, Queens, and Long Island

Same program. The address changes what the lease, the loan, and the borough ask to see.

New York

Many leases and loans name a limit, an additional insured, or a mortgagee. If the building has employees, workers compensation is generally required. Disability benefits and Paid Family Leave are separate. NYC landlord requirements.

Queens

Queens is part of New York City. We place commercial property and general liability for apartment buildings, mixed-use blocks, and small rentals there. City and state rules match the other boroughs. We read the lease or the loan against the policy. What is covered depends on the form.

Long Island

Nassau and Suffolk. Houses, two-families, and small apartment buildings. Long Island landlords.

What is the Real Estate Program?

It is the specialty for habitational accounts: landlords, mixed-use buildings, apartment buildings, co-ops, and condos. SimpleINSURE also places personal and commercial lines for other industries. What a policy covers depends on the form.

Do you place building insurance in Queens?

Yes. Queens is part of the New York work, with the other boroughs. Lender and lease requirements follow the loan, the lease, and New York City and state rules. The policy controls what is covered.

Is Long Island part of the program?

Yes. Nassau and Suffolk landlords and small buildings are part of it. A rental needs a landlord or building policy, not a homeowners policy. What is paid depends on the policy.

Does the program replace the other lines?

No. Personal and commercial lines are still placed, for every industry. Real estate is the specialty, not the only account.
