FAQ

What insurance does a New York business need?

It depends on the business, the lease, and whether there are employees. Most commercial accounts look at property, liability, and workers compensation when there are employees. We read what you have. The policy that is issued controls the coverage.

Do you only work in New York?

New York is the home market. SimpleINSURE Brokerage LLC is licensed in New York, Pennsylvania, and Florida (NY DFS BR-1984615). Principal also licensed in California. Those other license numbers are not published here. If an account needs a license we do not hold, we say so before a placement.

Does a businessowners policy include workers compensation?

Usually no. A businessowners policy packages property and liability for some smaller firms. Workers compensation, disability benefits, and Paid Family Leave are separate. Whether a firm qualifies depends on the insurer.

Is a certificate the same as coverage?

No. A certificate summarizes a policy. It does not add an additional insured or raise a limit. Those changes have to be on the policy.

Do you insure buildings outside New York?

The core market is New York. SimpleINSURE Brokerage LLC is licensed in New York, Pennsylvania, and Florida (NY DFS BR-1984615). Principal also licensed in California. Those other license numbers are not published here. A placement still depends on the insurer and on the license.

Can one broker handle the building and the business in it?

Often, yes. A mixed-use building and the business in it are usually separate policies. Each policy stands on its own. What each one covers depends on that policy.
