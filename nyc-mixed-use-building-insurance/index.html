The short answer

A mixed-use building is usually insured on a commercial package: the building, the owner's liability, and loss of rents, often with an umbrella on top.

Buildings we place

  • Walk-ups with one or two stores and apartments above
  • Corner buildings with a restaurant, deli or bodega on the ground floor
  • Buildings with offices or a medical office in the commercial space
  • Buildings with a vacant store between tenants
  • One building or several, owned in your name or in LLCs

What the program usually includes

  • Building. The structure against covered causes of loss, valued for what it would cost to rebuild.
  • Liability. Covered claims arising from the building, the common areas and the sidewalk.
  • Loss of rents. Residential and commercial rent lost while covered damage is repaired.
  • Equipment breakdown. A boiler or other building equipment that breaks down suddenly.
  • Ordinance or law. The added cost of meeting current code after a covered loss, up to the limit on the policy.
  • Umbrella. Higher liability limits above the primary policy.
  • Workers compensation and disability benefits. If you have a super or porter on payroll.

The commercial tenant matters

Your lease should ask each commercial tenant to carry its own liability policy and to name you, and often your managing agent, as additional insured.

Tell the insurer when a store changes hands. A new restaurant in a space that used to be a retail shop is a change in the building's risk.

New York City specifics

In New York City, the owner of a building is generally responsible for keeping the sidewalk in front of it safe.

Common questions

What insurance does a mixed-use building in NYC need?

Most owners carry building property, general liability, loss of rents and an umbrella, often in one commercial package. Many add equipment breakdown and ordinance or law. If there are building employees, New York workers compensation and disability benefits are required too.

Should my commercial tenant name me as additional insured?

Usually, yes. Most commercial leases ask the tenant to carry liability insurance and add the building owner, and often the managing agent, as additional insured. Collect a current certificate every year.

Does a restaurant tenant change my building insurance?

It can. A tenant with a cooking line is a different fire risk from an office or a store. Tell the insurer what the tenant does, and tell us when a new tenant moves in.

Is a mixed-use building insured as residential or commercial?

A mixed-use building with apartments and a store is usually written on a commercial policy, even when most of the units are apartments.

How much is mixed-use building insurance in NYC?

It depends on the building, the commercial tenants, the limits, the deductibles and the loss history. We do not publish prices. Send the declarations page for a review.

What should I send for a quote?

The address, the unit count and a rent roll, what each commercial tenant does, the year built, building system updates, current declarations pages, loss runs, and lender requirements.

Who insures mixed-use buildings in New York?

SimpleINSURE Brokerage LLC is a licensed New York insurance broker that places commercial property and general liability for mixed-use buildings in New York City and on Long Island. A mixed-use building is usually written on a commercial package, subject to underwriting and the policy terms.

Who is an insurance broker for a mixed-use building in Queens?

We work with owners of mixed-use buildings in Queens and across the five boroughs. If you have a store, office, or restaurant on the ground floor and apartments above, send the address, a rent roll, and what each commercial tenant does, and we will review it.

Do I need both commercial property and general liability for a mixed-use building in Queens?

Most owners carry both, often in one package. Property can cover the building against covered causes of loss. General liability can cover certain claims arising from the building, common areas, and the sidewalk, subject to the policy terms. Many owners also carry loss of rents and an umbrella.

Can you insure a mixed-use building on Long Island?

Yes, we place coverage for mixed-use and rental buildings in Nassau County and Suffolk County as well as in New York City. The same questions apply: what the commercial tenant does, the building's construction and updates, and what the lender asks for.

In New York City, is the building owner responsible for the sidewalk?

For most buildings, yes. New York City Administrative Code section 7-210 generally makes the owner responsible for keeping the sidewalk in front of the building reasonably safe. One-, two-, and three-family houses that are owner-occupied and used only as residences are treated differently. Sidewalk falls are a common liability claim on city buildings. Whether a policy responds depends on its terms. This is a plain summary, not legal advice.
