Coverage we place

What we can put in place.

Every account is different. Terms depend on underwriting, and the issued policy governs.

Business owners policy

Property and general liability packaged together for eligible small and mid-sized businesses.

Commercial property

Your building, improvements, equipment, and inventory, plus business income after a covered loss.

General liability

Claims of bodily injury or property damage to others arising from your premises or operations.

Professional liability

Errors and omissions coverage for firms that give advice or provide a professional service.

Cyber liability

Response costs and liability after a data breach, ransomware event, or network outage, depending on the form.

Workers compensation

Required for most New York employers. We also place New York disability and paid family leave.

Commercial auto

Vehicles owned by the business, plus hired and non-owned auto for employees who drive for work.

Employment practices

Claims of discrimination, harassment, or wrongful termination brought by employees.

Umbrella and excess

Additional liability limits above your general liability, auto, and employers liability.

Questions

Common questions.

Do I need a business owners policy or separate policies?

It depends on the size and type of business. Many smaller firms qualify for a package policy. Larger or specialized accounts may need property and liability written separately. We'll show you which applies.

Does general liability cover professional mistakes?

Usually not. General liability is written for bodily injury and property damage. Errors in professional advice or services are typically covered by a separate professional liability policy. The issued policy governs.
