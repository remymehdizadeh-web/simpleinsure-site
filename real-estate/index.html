Coverage we place

What we can put in place.

Every account is different. Terms depend on underwriting, and the issued policy governs.

Building property

The building itself, written on a replacement cost or other valuation basis.

Loss of rents

Rental income you lose while the building is repaired after a covered loss.

General liability

Injury claims from tenants, guests, and the public on the premises.

Umbrella and excess

Higher liability limits across one building or a schedule of buildings.

Ordinance or law

Extra cost to rebuild to current code after a loss, which older buildings often face.

Equipment breakdown

Boilers, HVAC, and electrical systems.

Workers compensation

For supers, porters, and other building staff, plus New York disability and paid family leave.

Dwelling policies

Coverage for one to four family rental homes.

Questions

Common questions.

Why does the age of my building matter?

Older buildings can cost more to repair because current building codes apply when you rebuild. Ordinance or law coverage addresses some of that cost. We review it with you.

Can you write a portfolio on one policy?

Often, yes. A schedule of buildings can be written together with a blanket or scheduled limit, depending on the insurer. We'll show you the options.
