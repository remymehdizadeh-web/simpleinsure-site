Questions

Common questions.

Are you tied to one insurance company?

No. SimpleINSURE Brokerage LLC is an independent broker and works with a range of insurance companies, including specialty markets. We represent you, not any single insurer.

How are you paid?

We are usually paid a commission by the insurance company that issues your policy. You can ask what we expect to receive on your policy, before or after you buy. The full disclosure is on our licenses and disclosures page.

What do I need for a quote?

Your current declarations pages are the place to start, plus your renewal date. For a business, we may also ask for payroll, revenue, and prior claims history. We'll tell you exactly what the insurers ask for.

Where do you work?

Our office is in Garden City on Long Island. We place insurance where we are licensed to do so, and this site states our New York license. If an account needs a license we do not hold, we will say so up front.

I'm already a client. Where do I go?

The Client Center handles certificates, ID cards, policy changes, claims, bills, and renewals. Or call (516) 226-7969.
