What does Nassau County require on a contractor's certificate of insurance?

Nassau County Department of Consumer Affairs sets the description of the work and the limits. The contractor insurance page quotes that sheet.

Do town building permits in Nassau need proof of workers comp?

Generally, yes. Town permit offices ask for proof of workers comp and disability benefits on New York State forms, or an exemption form, before they issue a permit. Check with your town's building department.
