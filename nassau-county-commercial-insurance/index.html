What does Nassau County require on a contractor's certificate of insurance?

Nassau County Consumer Affairs wants a current certificate, the insured name as it appears on the license application, and a description of the actual trade. It does not accept the words general contracting, remodeling, carpentry, or home improvement in place of that description. A home improvement license shows bodily injury of $250,000/$500,000 and property damage of $100,000. The certificate holder is Nassau County Department of Consumer Affairs, 240 Old County Road, 3rd Floor, Mineola, New York 11501. If you subcontract, the county also requires liability insurance for subcontracting. Other license types have different limits. The full sheet is on the Nassau County insurance requirements page. Those limits are also listed on the contractor insurance page.

Do town building permits in Nassau need proof of workers comp?

Generally, yes. Town permit offices ask for proof of workers comp and disability benefits on New York State forms, or an exemption form, before they issue a permit. Check with your town's building department.
