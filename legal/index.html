Producer compensation disclosure

SimpleINSURE Brokerage LLC is an insurance producer licensed by the State of New York. When you work with us, we act as your insurance broker: we represent you in finding coverage and presenting options from insurers, and we do not act for any single insurer.

We are usually paid a commission by the insurance company that issues your policy, generally a percentage of the premium. We may also receive other compensation from insurers or from third parties, such as wholesale brokers or managing general agents, including contingent or bonus payments.

The compensation we receive may vary depending on a number of factors, including the insurance contract and the insurance company you select, the volume of business we place with an insurer, and the profitability of that business.

You may request information about the compensation we expect to receive from your purchase, and about compensation we would have received on any alternative quotes we presented to you. Ask us before or after you buy, and we will provide it.

This notice is also provided at or before the time you apply for coverage. It is a general description and not a quote for your account.

Not an offer or a binder

Descriptions on this website are general. They do not amend a policy and they are not an offer to insure. Coverage is subject to underwriting and to the forms that are issued. The policy governs.

Privacy

Forms and email

Your answers and files are emailed to SimpleINSURE through our form service, FormSubmit. FormSubmit keeps form answers for up to 30 days and says it does not keep uploaded files. Nothing is stored on this website. FormSubmit is operated by Devro LABS, which acts as our form processor. If a form cannot be sent, the page offers to open an email draft in your own mail program, addressed to info@simpleinsure.co instead. Ordinary email is not encrypted. Do not send Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, dates of birth, bank account or payment card numbers, or medical information by email, and do not attach a document that shows them. If a declarations page or application shows that kind of information, black it out or call (516) 226-7969 and we will arrange another way to send it.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics, a service of Google LLC, to count visits and see which pages are used. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and receives information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type. We use this only in aggregate to improve the site. We do not sell personal information. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Advertising

We use the Google Ads tag and cookies to measure ad performance. We do not send your name, email, phone number, policy details, or files to Google. You can review Google's ad settings and policies at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How we use what you send

If you email or call, we use what you send to respond, to obtain quotes and coverage, and to service your account. To do that we share it with insurance companies, wholesale brokers and others involved in placing or servicing your insurance, and otherwise only as permitted or required by law.

Privacy notice for personal insurance customers

We collect nonpublic personal information about you from your applications and other forms; your transactions with us and with insurance companies; and, with your authorization where required, consumer reporting agencies and motor vehicle records. We do not disclose nonpublic personal information about our customers or former customers to anyone except as permitted by law, for example to insurance companies and others as needed to place, service or process your insurance, to comply with law, or with your consent. We do not share it with nonaffiliated third parties for their own marketing. We restrict access to nonpublic personal information to those who need it to provide services to you, and we maintain physical, electronic and procedural safeguards that comply with applicable New York requirements to protect it. A copy of this notice is provided when you obtain personal insurance through us, and annually as required.
