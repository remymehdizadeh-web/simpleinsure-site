Does landlord insurance cover loss of rent?

It can, if loss of rents is on the policy and a covered cause, such as a fire, makes units unlivable. It does not pay when a tenant stops paying.

What is loss of rents coverage?

It replaces rent lost while covered damage is repaired, for the limit or the period on the declarations page.

Does landlord insurance cover tenant damage?

Sudden accidental damage from a covered cause usually is. Wear and tear, and often intentional tenant damage, is not.

What is ordinance or law coverage, and why does an older building need it?

After a covered loss, repairs may have to meet current code. Ordinance or law coverage can pay that extra cost, up to its limit.

Does landlord insurance cover a tenant's belongings?

Tenants need their own renters policy, and many leases require one.

Landlord vs homeowners insurance: what is the difference?

A homeowners policy is for a home you live in. A rental needs a policy written for landlord use.

My building insurance was non-renewed. What now?

Send the notice, loss runs, and recent updates, and tell your lender before it places coverage. Notice periods depend on the policy.

What to send

  • Declarations pages and loss runs
  • Rent roll and updates to the roof, boiler, plumbing, and electrical
  • The lender's written requirements
