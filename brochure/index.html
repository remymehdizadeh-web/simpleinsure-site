Agency overview

A licensed brokerage that reads the policy, explains it plainly, and shops the market when you ask us to.

What we place

Commercial lines

  • Building and landlord packages
  • Restaurant and liquor packages
  • Contractor GL and job certificates
  • Business owners policies (BOP)
  • Commercial auto and hired or non-owned
  • Umbrella / excess
  • Workers compensation (as available)

Personal lines

  • Homeowners, condo / co-op
  • Personal auto
  • Personal umbrella
  • Renters

For homeowners on Long Island and in the city.

Service

  • A plain-language review of the policies you already have
  • Certificates for lenders, tenants and job sites
  • Claims advocacy when something goes wrong

How we work

01 Share what you have. Send the policies or declarations pages you already have. Even a photo of the first page works.

02 We review every policy. A team reads limits, deductibles, building values, and gaps, then marks what we see in plain English.

03 Shop when you ask. We shop admitted and specialty markets when you want options, with no obligation to move anything.

04 Ongoing service. Fast certificates when the policy allows, and a broker who picks up the phone.

Markets

We have access to admitted carriers and specialty and excess and surplus markets, both directly and through established wholesale partners.

Markets we can access include Chubb, Hiscox, Foremost, RLI, THREE by Berkshire Hathaway, Attune, Coterie, and Plymouth Rock.
