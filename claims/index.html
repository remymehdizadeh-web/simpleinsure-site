Claims

Report a claim.

Call 911 if someone is hurt. Then call the insurer on the policy. Call us and we will help. We do not decide the claim.

Call (516) 226-7969Client center

What to do

  1. If anyone is hurt or in danger, call 911.
  2. Call the claim number on the policy or the ID card. A note to us is not notice to the insurance company.
  3. Call us at (516) 226-7969, Mon to Fri, 9 to 6 ET. If it is after hours and the loss is urgent, use the insurer claim line first.
  4. Write down the date, the address, and what happened. Photos help. Do not write who was at fault.
  5. Clients can send the note from the client center. We help you report it. The insurance company decides whether it is covered, and whether anything is paid.

Have this ready

  • Policy number, if you have it
  • Date and location
  • What was damaged, or who was hurt
  • A police or incident number, if there is one
  • Photos, with Social Security, license, birth date, and bank or card numbers blacked out

Workplace injuries

A workplace injury is reported to the employer and to the workers compensation insurer on the policy. Disability benefits and Paid Family Leave go on those policies, not on the building policy. The insurer decides the claim.

What we do not do

  • We do not promise that a claim will be paid
  • We do not promise a timeline
  • We do not speak for the insurance company
If I email SimpleINSURE, is the claim reported?

No. A note to us is not notice to the insurance company. The policy requires the loss to be reported to the insurer. We will help you do that.

Who decides if a claim is covered?

The insurance company that issued the policy. We do not decide coverage, and we do not promise payment.

What if the loss is happening now?

If anyone is in danger, call 911. Then call the claim number on the policy. Call us at (516) 226-7969, Mon to Fri, 9 to 6 ET.
