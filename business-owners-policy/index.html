What a businessowners policy (BOP) covers in New York

Property, lost income, and liability in one policy.

Send your policy(516) 226-7969

What does BOP stand for?

BOP stands for businessowners policy. It packages property, lost income, and general liability for many smaller businesses. It does not include every coverage a business can need. Workers compensation, for example, is separate.

What does a businessowners policy cover?

A businessowners policy generally covers your building or business property, income lost after a covered loss, and liability for injury or damage others say you caused. Limits and exclusions are on the form you buy. This is a general summary.

Does a business owners policy include workers comp?

No. Workers compensation is its own policy. In New York, disability benefits are separate too. If you have employees, you still need those policies beside the businessowners policy. The Workers' Compensation Board explains workers compensation coverage requirements.

What does a BOP not cover?

A businessowners policy does not include workers compensation, commercial auto, professional liability, flood, or earthquake. Those are separate policies when you need them. Read the exclusions on the form.

BOP vs general liability: what is the difference?

A businessowners policy is general liability plus property and lost income. General liability alone pays for injury or damage you cause to other people, up to its limit, when the claim is covered. It does not insure your building, your equipment, your stock, or income lost after a property loss. Workers compensation, vehicles, professional liability, flood, and earthquake stay separate.

Is a BOP the same as general liability?

No. General liability is only the part that responds to injury or damage you cause to others. A businessowners policy includes that liability and also covers your property and income you lose after a covered property loss. If a business does not qualify for a businessowners policy, those parts can be bought as separate policies.

Do I qualify for a BOP?

It depends on the type of business, the size, the building, and the claims. Some businesses fit a businessowners policy. Others buy property and liability separately. We can tell you which one an insurer will offer after we see the operation.
