How to refer

  1. Tell your client you are introducing SimpleINSURE, or ask us to say you sent them.
  2. Send their name, best phone or email, and a one-line note on what they need.
  3. A licensed broker at SimpleINSURE replies during business hours (Mon to Fri, 9 to 6 ET).
  4. With your client's OK, we keep you posted on status. We do not share their policy details without permission.
