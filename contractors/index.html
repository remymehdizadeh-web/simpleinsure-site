Why New York is different. New York Labor Law section 240(1) can make owners and contractors liable for certain elevation-related injuries regardless of fault, and section 241(6) places nondelegable site-safety duties on them. That shapes how contractor liability is written here, including the exclusions some policies carry. We read those exclusions with you before coverage is bound.

Questions we get

Can you match the insurance requirements in my contract?

Send us the contract or the requirements page. We'll check the limits and endorsements it asks for against what you have, and tell you what changes, if any, are needed.

Do I need workers comp if I only use subcontractors?

It depends on how the subcontractors are set up and whether they carry their own coverage. In New York construction, workers are presumed to be employees unless they meet the independent contractor or separate business test. A contractor can be liable for comp for a sub's workers if the sub has no coverage, and an audit can charge you for subs without certificates. We'll go through it with you.
