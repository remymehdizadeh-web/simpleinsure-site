Do you insure buildings outside New York?

The core market is New York. The firm license published here is New York, NY DFS BR-1984615. The principal also holds individual producer licenses in Pennsylvania, Florida, and California. Those numbers are not published here. A placement still depends on the insurer and on the license.

Can one broker handle the building and the business in it?

Often, yes. A mixed-use building and the business in it are usually separate policies. Each policy stands on its own. What each one covers depends on that policy.
