Coverage we place

Every account is different. Terms depend on underwriting, and the issued policy governs.

Property
Your building or improvements, kitchen equipment, furniture, and inventory.
Business income
Lost income and continuing expenses while you are closed after a covered loss.
General liability
Customer slip-and-fall and other third-party injury or damage claims.
Food spoilage and equipment breakdown
Spoiled stock after a power or cooler failure, and breakdown of refrigeration and kitchen equipment, depending on the form.
Workers compensation
Required for most New York employers, plus New York disability and paid family leave.
Hired and non-owned auto
Liability when staff use their own cars for deliveries or errands.
Employment practices
Claims of discrimination, harassment, or wrongful termination brought by employees.
Umbrella
Additional liability limits above your general liability, auto, and employers liability.

Who we work with

  • Current declarations pages and your renewal date

Questions we get

What does business income cover after a fire?

It depends on the policy wording, limit, and waiting period. In general it covers lost income and continuing expenses while you are closed after a covered loss. We read your form with you.
