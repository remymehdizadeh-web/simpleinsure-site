What changed on June 17, 2026

On June 17, 2026, Chapter 696 of the Laws of 2025 took effect. It removed the old $1.25 million peer-to-peer liability floor.

Turo says NY host default liability is now $75,000 per person / $150,000 per accident, with a top plan of $300,000 combined. Those dollar figures are Turo's description of its New York host plans. They are not a new limit written into the statute.

Turo also describes physical damage during a trip as contractual reimbursement, with a deductible that depends on the plan. That reimbursement is not the host's own auto policy.

  • Turo: insurance changes for New York
  • New York Senate Bill S5941B (Chapter 696 of the Laws of 2025)

Trip and off trip

During a trip, the host plan is the one Turo describes above. Off trip, the car is still yours. Cleaning, an empty delivery, a maintenance run, and time in a lot are not part of the trip.

A personal auto policy often excludes a car while it is listed for sharing. New York Insurance Law section 3459 lets an owner's policy exclude the sharing period. The form and the endorsements on your policy control. Ask before you assume a personal policy applies.

Coverage we look for:

  • Commercial auto or fleet. Liability and physical damage on cars you own that are listed on Turo.
  • Excess or umbrella liability. Limits above the trip limits Turo describes, and above a commercial auto policy when the car is off trip.
  • Hired and non-owned auto. For a cohost, a detailer, or an employee who drives.
  • An LLC as named insured. A policy that names the operating company, rather than leaving the cars on a personal auto policy.

Does personal auto insurance cover a car listed on Turo in New York?

Often it does not, during the time the car is shared. New York Insurance Law section 3459 lets an owner's policy exclude the sharing period. Your policy form and endorsements control. This is not a decision on your policy. You can send the declarations page with the review request.

What insurance do Turo hosts need when the car is not on a trip?

Turo's trip plan does not apply to cleaning, an empty delivery, a maintenance run, or the time the car sits in a lot. Hosts who run this as a business usually look for commercial auto while the car is off trip, physical damage on the owned cars, hired and non-owned auto if someone else drives, and excess liability.
